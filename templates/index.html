How the bridge works

  1. Traditional layer. Every recorded ethnobotanical use of the plant, with the country of record and the source citation where the database provides one.
  2. Science layer. The phytochemical constituents recorded for the plant, and the biological activities recorded for each constituent.
  3. Bridge. Each traditional use is matched against those constituent activities. Matches are labelled direct or related, never counted as proof of efficacy.
  4. Verify. Every claim carries a PubMed link-out so you can read the underlying literature yourself.
