Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

What we collect

Creating an account stores your email address and a salted, irreversible hash of your password. Passwords are never stored or logged in readable form. If you save plants or write notes, those are stored with your account only.

What we do not collect

No advertising cookies, no analytics, no third-party trackers, and no profiling. The evidence database (plant uses and constituents) is public-domain USDA data and is served read-only.

How your data is used

Only to sign you in and to show you your own saved plants and notes. It is never sold, shared, or used to train anything.

Your controls

You can export everything tied to your account (/api/auth/export) or delete your account and all of its data from the saved-plants page at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the methodology page for how to reach the maintainer.
